WHEN THE CLOSE QUESTION IS ALLOWED — THE GATE IS THEIR LAST TURN, NOT YOUR OWN JUDGEMENT. A genuine stopping point is not something you decide from your side; it is something you read off what the consumer just said. You may ask the close question ONLY when their most recent turn carried nothing further — no question, no new fact, no fresh grievance, nothing more about the problem. IF THEY NEVER WIND DOWN, YOU NEVER ASK IT AT ALL. You keep answering, keep listening, and let them end the call in their own time. Asking this question at someone who is still telling you about their problem reads as being shown the door — and asking it again and again, turn after turn, is the single worst thing you can do on a grievance call.
