# Commercial LPG knowledge base (IVRS press 9)

The facts Vaani may state on the commercial line. Every fact here comes from an official source
(BPCL, commerciallpg.in, Bharatgas, MoPNG, PIB, BIS, the Gazette), checked on 2026-10-23. Prompts take
their commercial facts from this file and from nothing else. A fact that is not here is not stated.

Sources are listed at the end of each section. Open points that BPCL or the team still has to answer
are NOT in this file; they live in [../BPCL_QUESTIONS.md](../BPCL_QUESTIONS.md).

## 1. What this line is

- Commercial and industrial LPG customers book their cylinders on Bharat Petroleum's commercial IVRS.
  Pressing 9 on that IVRS brings them to Vaani for any query or complaint about their commercial
  connection.
- Who the customers are: "Hotels & Restaurants, Hospitals & Healthcare, Educational Institutions,
  Government Departments, PSUs, Private Enterprises, Canteens & Cafeterias, Industrial Units".
  Also caterers, dhabas, bakeries, community kitchens, factories and process industries.
- The caller may be the owner, a manager, or staff of the business.
- Commercial LPG is sold at the non-domestic price as decided by the oil companies. It carries no
  subsidy.

Sources: commerciallpg.in home ("Who we serve"); BPCL Citizen's Charter Jan 2026, ch.1 3.3.

## 2. Products

- Commercial pack sizes: 5 kg, 19 kg (the standard commercial cylinder), 35 kg (medium
  industrial cylinder) and 47.5 kg (industrial cylinder).
- LOT versions: 19 kg LOT, 35 kg LOT, 47.5 kg LOT.
  - In plain words: a normal commercial cylinder gives out gas from the top (vapour off-take). A
    LOT (liquid off-take) cylinder sends liquid LPG into a vaporiser.
  - LOT is for industrial users who need a high flow of gas for their processes. Liquid off-take
    installations are provided only in industrial premises.
- Hippo: a large LOT unit for industrial and high-volume commercial users. It sits between
  cylinder systems and bulk installations, saves space, and has a gauge that shows how much gas is
  left. The size is not stated: the portal says both 422 kg and 425 kg.
- Bulk LPG: supplied in bulk tankers for industries and large properties.
- BMCG, Bharat Metal Cutting Gas:
  - A special LPG blend for metal cutting, welding, brazing, heating and bending. It replaces
    acetylene.
  - Its cylinder pressure is about a third of acetylene's, which makes it safer. It is non-toxic and
    gives no torch backfire.
  - It cuts at speeds equal to acetylene, uses less oxygen and forms less slag.
  - Sizes: 19, 35 and 47.5 kg.
- 5 kg FTL (Free Trade LPG, Bharatgas Mini):
  - A 5 kg cylinder bought with only a proof of identity, at any LPG distributor, BPCL fuel
    station or select general store.
  - The first purchase includes the cylinder, regulator and admin charges; after that each refill
    costs only the gas. Once bought, the cylinder belongs to the customer.
  - The commercial portal lists "5 kg FTL for migrant labourers" as a sector.
- LPG is clean-burning, made mainly of propane and butane, with a high calorific value. It is
  heavier than air, so a leak settles low.

Sources: commerciallpg.in FAQ, Submit Request form and Catalogue; bharatpetroleum.in Bharat Metal
Cutting Gas, Bharatgas Mini, Industrial & Commercial LPG pages; my.ebharatgas.com BuyCylinder; IS 6044
(Part 1):2013 clause 1.2.

## 3. New commercial supply (a new connection, more supply, a new location)

- **How to apply.** Online on the BPCL commercial LPG portal, www.commerciallpg.in, using Submit
  Request.
  - No registration is needed; the mobile number is verified by OTP.
  - Any commercial or industrial entity in India can apply: hotels, restaurants, hospitals,
    educational institutions, government departments, PSUs, private enterprises, factories and
    canteens.
- **What the form asks.**
  - Organisation name and type, sector, address and pincode.
  - Whether they already have a Bharatgas connection (and its consumer number).
  - Contact person, mobile and email; PAN; GST status and number; current supplier and current
    fuel.
  - Pack size, number of cylinders and monthly consumption, frequency (one time, weekly, monthly,
    long-term tie-up), and end use.
  - One application can list several supply locations. For each location the form asks whether
    PNG is available in the area and the customer's PNG status.
- **Documents to upload.** A supporting document, the last invoice copy, a photo of the business
  premises, and a photo of the place where the LPG cylinders are kept. Each file can be JPG, PNG,
  PDF or DOC, up to 5 MB.
- **What happens next.**
  1. The request is submitted, and a unique Request ID is given at once.
  2. The ED Committee (Executive Director Committee) reviews it and may ask for more details.
  3. Approved requests go automatically to the Sales Officer and the distributor for that
     location.
  4. The Sales Officer confirms the supply recommendation, and the distributor delivers.
- **Updates.** The customer gets an SMS at every status change on the mobile used for the request.
- **Tracking.** On the portal's Track Request page, with the mobile number used for the request
  and an OTP (valid for 10 minutes). All of that mobile's requests are shown there.
- **Cancelling.** A request can be cancelled from the Track Request page, after the OTP, by choosing
  a reason.
- **Status names the portal shows.** Under Consideration, Seek More Details, Approved by ED,
  Rejected by ED, Under SO Review, Recommended for Supply, Not Recommended for Supply, Delivered,
  Not Delivered.
- **No guarantee.** The form's declaration says submitting a request does not guarantee approval,
  allotment or supply, and the request is processed under Government of India rules at the time of
  approval.
- **Customers moving from another company.** The form's "current supplier" list includes other
  companies (Indian Oil, Hindustan Petroleum, private suppliers), so they apply the same way.

Sources: commerciallpg.in home ("How It Works"), FAQ, Submit Request, Track Request.

## 4. Booking a refill

- A commercial customer books refills on Bharat Petroleum's commercial IVRS, the line they called
  from.
- No booking quota, limit or gap is published for commercial customers. Whether a booking can be
  made comes only from the customer's own data and the tool Results, never from a rule in a prompt.

Source: commerciallpg.in ("the Distributor executes delivery").

## 6. Price, GST and payment

- **Price.** The commercial price is market-determined, linked to international benchmarks, and
  revised every month. It differs by state, district and area.
  - The customer can see the current price for their area on www.commerciallpg.in, on the LPG
    Prices page, by choosing the state, sales district and area.
  - Vaani never quotes a price figure from memory.
- **GST.** Commercial LPG carries 18% GST. A GST-registered business can claim input tax credit,
  subject to its own eligibility.
- **Payment.** Customers can pay online through the Bharat Petroleum website and app, and by UPI,
  as well as on delivery. Payment methods are the same as on the domestic line; there is no subsidy
  part.
- **Receipt.** The customer receives an SMS with the cash memo number and amount.

Sources: PIB 01 Jan 2026 and 07 Jun 2026; BPCL Charter Jan 2026 ch.1 3.3 and 5.9; commerciallpg.in
LPG Prices and Fuel Savings Calculator; my.ebharatgas.com SMS page.

## 7. Safety at a commercial installation (IS 6044 Part 1 and Part 2, BPCL safety advice)

General advice only. For anything about the design, size or approval of an installation, the
customer follows their supplier's instructions and applicable rules.

- Cylinders stand upright with the valve on top, on a concrete or brick floor, in a ventilated
  place, away from heat such as steam pipes, boilers and burners.
- Cylinders are never kept or used below ground level, in a cellar or a basement.
- Cylinders holding more than 20 kg of gas are not kept on floors above ground level.
- Cylinders are kept at least 1 metre away from drains, culverts and openings that lead below
  ground.
- An unused cylinder, full or empty, always has its safety cap on.
- Flexible hose:
  - The cylinder and the appliance are in the same room.
  - The hose is normally no longer than 2 metres.
  - It never passes through a door, window, wall, partition, ceiling or floor.
- A manifold has change-over valves so gas does not escape while cylinders are changed.
- A main valve is fitted in the gas pipe near where it enters the building.
- Leaks are checked with soap solution, never with a flame.
- A defective pipe or fitting is replaced, not repaired in place.
- Dry powder or carbon dioxide fire extinguishers are kept near the cylinder installation, with two
  buckets of sand and two of water nearby.
- Bulk storage tanks and their first regulators are always outside the building.
- The supplier instructs the customer on how the system works, how to recognise a leak, and what to
  do in a leak, a fire or a failure of any part.

Sources: IS 6044 (Part 1):2000 with Amendment 1 (2008), clauses 3.4, 4.1.8 to 4.1.14, 5.3, 8.1.1,
8.1.2, 8.6, 9.2 to 9.4, 10.1; IS 6044 (Part 2):2001 clause 3.1.1; BPCL Charter Jan 2026 ch.1 9.1.

## 8. Gas leak or fire (emergency)

- **1906.** The all-India LPG emergency helpline, toll free, 24 hours, for LPG leakage. The
  distributor's service for a leakage complaint is free of charge.
- **What to do at once in a leak or a smell of gas:**
  1. Turn the regulator knob to OFF.
  2. Put out every flame nearby.
  3. Do not light a match or lighter, and do not switch any electrical switch on or off (switches
     make sparks). Keep mobile phones away.
  4. Open all doors and windows.
  5. Call the emergency helpline 1906.

Sources: MoPNG Emergency Helpline page; BPCL Charter Jan 2026 ch.1 8.1, 8.2, 9.2(c).

## 9. Uses of LPG in industry (commerciallpg.in industry pages)

Vaani may describe these uses. She does not quote savings, efficiency or cost figures.

- **Hotels:** cooking, hot water and steam for laundry, LPG tandoors and grills; bulk piped supply
  for large properties.
- **Baking:** industrial ovens for biscuits, rusk, cakes, pastry and bread.
- **Pharmaceuticals:** bottle drying, and steam for sterilisation.
- **Heat treatment:** annealing, hardening, tempering, normalising, and preheating before
  welding.
- **Automobile:** die casting, forging, heat treatment, powder coating, paint baking and tyre
  curing.
- **Die casting:** melting aluminium.
- **Forging:** heating steel billets.
- **Powder coating:** curing.
- **Galvanisation:** zinc coating of steel.
- **Glass:** melting, forming and annealing.
- **Ceramics:** kilns for tiles, sanitary ware and crockery.
- **Hot mix plants:** heating bitumen for roads.
- **Expanded polystyrene:** used as a blowing agent.
- **Aerosols:** propellant for sprays.
- **Poultry:** brooding for chicks.
- **Agriculture:** grain, fruit, coffee and seed drying, tobacco curing and flame weeding.
- **Tea:** withering and drying tea leaves.
- **Textiles:** singeing, calendaring, drying and steam.
- **Chemicals:** feedstock.
- **LPG forklifts.**
- **Gas heat pumps.**
- **Synthetic natural gas:** LPG mixed with air as a backup to natural gas.

The portal also has a Fuel Savings Calculator for comparing a current fuel with LPG.

Source: commerciallpg.in industry pages and fuel-savings page.
