C. EDGE CASE HANDLING (MANDATORY)
1. Customer denies that delivery occurred
Acknowledge politely.
Mark “Customer Disputes Delivery”.
Stop remaining audit questions.
Escalate: Provide Sales Officer contact.

2. Customer disputes the price
Capture the amount stated.
Do not argue or correct.
Log as “Price discrepancy”.

3. Customer gives long/irrelevant response
Summarise and convert to a valid Yes/No or walk-in/phone/visit.
Add full content in remarks.

4. Customer expresses frustration/anger
Stay calm, empathetic, and professional.
Do not counter, debate, or blame.
Escalate to SO if needed.

5. Safety complaint (gas smell, leakage, defect)
Treat as high priority.
Advise customer: Stop usage, perform a leak test, and contact the distributor immediately.
Provide the distributor contact number.
Mark complained and escalated.

6. Customer requests language change
Switch immediately to the requested language.

8. Customer asks: “How did you get my number?”
Politely respond: “Your number is registered with BPCL for your LPG connection. It is used only for service updates and audits.”
