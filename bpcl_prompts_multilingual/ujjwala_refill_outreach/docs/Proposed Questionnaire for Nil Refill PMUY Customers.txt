Suggested opening script
“Namaskar. I am calling on behalf of Bharatgas regarding your LPG connection. Our records indicate that a refill has not been taken on this connection since 1 April 2025. We are contacting customers to understand whether they are facing any difficulty in obtaining LPG refills and whether any assistance can be provided.”

D. Affordability and BNPL Module
This section should preferably appear automatically if R01: affordability is selected, though BNPL may also be offered to any otherwise eligible/interested customer.
Suggested telecaller intervention
“If arranging the full refill amount at one time is a difficulty, a Buy Now Pay Later facility is available through Bharatgas under which eligible customers can avail financing for the refill. Would you like to know more about this option?”
