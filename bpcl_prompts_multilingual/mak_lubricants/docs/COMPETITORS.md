# Competitor research — two-wheeler oil, India (2026-10-03)

Background for the **COMPETITOR BRANDS** block in `prompts/Default/Default.txt`.

**None of this goes into the prompt.** Urja deliberately holds no competitor facts — only brand-name
recognition — so that she cannot invent a grade, a price or a claim. This file exists so that *we*
can check that the reasons she does give are true, and so **nobody has to run this research again**.
It is deliberately complete, including the marketing claims and every source URL, so the next person
can verify or refresh a single figure without starting over.

Prices are approximate street prices from Amazon.in / Flipkart / Moglix / IndiaMART, **October 2026**.
They move constantly, online listings are heavily discounted against MRP, and several figures are
third-party-seller quotes. Treat every figure as indicative. Anything uncertain is flagged inline.

---

## 1. Why this came up

BPCL does little consumer advertising for मेक, while Castrol, Gulf, Servo and Shell advertise heavily.
Callers therefore name a competitor's product out of habit on a line they rang *because it is Bharat
Petroleum's*. Client requirement (user, 2026-10-03): Urja must treat a competitor name as a **cue to
sell मेक**, never as a topic to discuss. The user chose **Castrol, Servo and Gulf** as the focus —
Castrol for brand pull, Servo and Gulf because the brief is Indian competitors for Indian callers.
Mobil, Shell and Motul are in the prompt's recognition list only, because callers do say them.

## 2. Market position

| Brand | Owner | Position in Indian 2W oil |
|---|---|---|
| **Castrol** | BP (foreign) | Leads the 2W segment on brand pull (~27% of the segment). Activ and POWER1 are household names. |
| **Servo** | Indian Oil, PSU | India's largest lubricant brand overall — ~₹8,000 crore, **>27% of the finished-lubes market** (IOCL's own figure); some sources put IOCL production share near 40%. मेक's direct mirror: PSU, pump distribution, same mechanic channel. In 2W its edge is distribution, not brand premium. |
| **Gulf** | Gulf Oil Lubricants India, Hinduja (Indian-listed) | **#2 private player after Castrol**, ~7%+ of the bazaar/replacement market, fastest share gainer since ~FY15. Over-indexed in 2W. Competes on price-per-litre plus celebrity visibility. |
| Mobil | ExxonMobil | **Niche** in 2W; known in India for car oil. Publicly pushing to grow 2W via ~500 branded stores and 50+ distributors, but still a minority player. |
| Shell | Shell | AX5 mainstream volume, AX7 the hero line, Ultra premium. |
| Motul | Motul | Premium/enthusiast. The default for RE 650 twins, KTM, and performance bikes. |

No credible head-to-head 2W-only share split between **Servo and मेक** could be found — treat any
specific number for that as unverified.

1. **"Newer formula" is not a universal win.** Gulf Pride and Syntrac are already API SP, as are

   never wrong — but do not strengthen the wording.

   one, and it is *the* volume commuter grade. Per the "only where मेक wins" rule, Urja never enters
   that comparison: she gives the grade BLOCK 6 lists for the caller's actual vehicle.
3. **Cashback is not unique to मेक.** Mechanic loyalty and cashback schemes are common in this
   industry. "Only मेक gives this" could be contradicted by an enrolled mechanic mid-call and would
   cost her credibility. She says what मेक gives, never what others do not.
4. **Price is not a win at the premium end.** Gulf Syntrac 1 L runs ~₹650–900 against next synth at
   ₹903. मेक wins on value in the commuter range, not everywhere.
