THE LABEL IS THE ONLY SPEAKER EVIDENCE. Attribute every line by its label, never by its content.
CONSECUTIVE LINES WITH THE SAME LABEL ARE ONE TURN. A near-duplicate line from the same speaker is a transcription artefact (a partial line followed by the complete one), not a repetition.
TRANSCRIPTION IS IMPERFECT. Garbled or wrong-sounding words are nobody's fault. Where a line is too corrupted to read, treat its content as absent; never guess what it meant.
HINDI, HINGLISH, HINDI IN LATIN LETTERS AND OTHER INDIAN LANGUAGES ARE ALL NORMAL SPEECH HERE.
SYSTEM OR TOOL TEXT IS NOT SPEECH. A line that is plainly a platform notice, a dialling result, a tool call or an error, under either label, is not a turn and is never evidence.

THE RULES FOR EVERY FIELD
- RECORD WHAT THE CUSTOMER SAID, NOT WHAT URJA PITCHED. A decision is the customer's own words. Urja recommending ₹5,00,000 is not the customer agreeing to it. Only an affirmative from the customer on that very thing is a yes: "हाँ", "जी", "ठीक है", "हो जाएगा", "कर देंगे", "कर दूँगा", "done" said in reply to it.
- WHEN UNSURE, TAKE THE WEAKER VALUE. A vague "देखते हैं" is later, not agreed. A topic Urja raised that the customer never answered is heard, not agreed. A topic never raised is notDiscussed.
- NEVER INVENT. Never a figure, date, name, amount, partner or reason that is not in the transcript. If the customer names an amount, copy it; never round it and never calculate one.
- EVERY VALUE IS A PLAIN STRING. Never null, never a number type, never an empty string, never a nested object, never an array. Where a field has nothing to record, use the value the field definition gives for that case.
- ENUM FIELDS take exactly one of the values listed, spelled exactly as written.
- TEXT FIELDS are written in plain English unless the field says otherwise, on one line, with no line breaks.
- AMOUNTS in amount fields are digits only in rupees, with no commas, no symbol and no words: "500000", "150000", "200000". If the customer agreed but named no amount of their own and accepted Urja's figure, record Urja's figure that they accepted.
